What you get

A real station, without the station

Record and trim on any phone

A recorder in the browser with a level meter, pause and resume, and a waveform to cut silences and mistakes. Recordings are compressed to about 4 MB per 10 minutes, kind to mobile data.

Shows that start on time

Schedule a programme and it goes out within a second of its slot. Between shows, your playlist keeps the station alive.

A page made for sharing

Every station gets its own page with a player, live status and a WhatsApp button. Links show your name and logo in the chat.

Light on data

Listeners use about 29 MB an hour. Pausing stops the download, and dropped connections reconnect on their own.

Your station stands alone

Each station runs on its own engine, so another station's problem never takes yours off air.

    Questions

    Straight answers

    Do my listeners need an app?

    No. They open your station's link in any browser on a phone or computer and press play.

    Do I need a laptop or a studio?

    No. A smartphone and a quiet room are enough to start. A laptop or a better microphone helps, but isn't required.

    Can I play music?

    Talk, news, faith, sport and education need no music licence. Commercial music needs permission from the rights holders; we'll guide stations on this and are working on an opt-in library of independent artists.

    Do I need a broadcasting licence?

    Rules for internet broadcasting in Nigeria are changing, and the NBC has said online broadcasters should register. We're confirming exactly what applies and will help stations with what they need.

    Who owns my programmes?

    You do. Your recordings, your audience, your station name.
